# Area Rugs

Looking for area rugs for the Tudor Revival house in Capitol Hill. Budget: under $1k per rug.

## Online — Best Value

**Vintage / Handmade**
- Revival Rugs — vintage Turkish rugs, most under $1k, curated with room photos
- Etsy — huge selection of vintage Turkish village rugs and handmade pieces; many ship from Turkey at great prices
- Lolo Rugs & Gifts — vintage Persian and Turkish, many under $1k
- Canvello — handmade rugs at auction prices

**Discount New**
- RugsUSA — frequent 70-80% off sales, solid quality
- Boutique Rugs — similar deep discount model
- Ruggable — washable rugs, good for high-traffic areas
- West Elm / Article — periodic sales on decent quality rugs

**Curated Secondhand**
- Chairish — vetted vintage, filter by price
- 1stDibs — "under $1,000" filter; good for Oushak and kilims
- eBay — estate auction rugs regularly go well under $1k

## Local Seattle

- Pacific Galleries (SoDo) — auction house, estate pieces
- Old Seattle Rug (Fremont) — antique/vintage oriental rugs
- Ballard Consignment Store
- Rug stores on Aurora Ave
- Facebook Marketplace / OfferUp / Craigslist

## Style Notes
- Persian, Turkish, or Oushak rugs pair well with Tudor Revival interiors
- Vintage Turkish Oushak and Persian Heriz tend to be best value under $1k
