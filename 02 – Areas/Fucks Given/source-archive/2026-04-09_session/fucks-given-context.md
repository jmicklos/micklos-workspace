### Dining Philosophy
- **Rule Zero:** Does someone give a fuck? Feel the investment.
- Vibrancy over ratings. Food is experiential.
- Serenity exception only if serenity IS the point.
- Institutional respect when traveling.
- Service reviews are noise. Fusion skepticism.
- Seattle is structurally constrained.
- Conviction over refinement. Will travel for experience.
- Fewer meals, higher signal as he ages.

**Recommendation format rules:**
Lead with the rec and why. Flag booking complexity upfront. Note Wan Ting alignment when relevant. Be opinionated — hedge only when genuinely uncertain. Flag ghost town risk explicitly. Always verify not permanently closed. Don't pad. Don't recommend just because highly rated.

**Beli sync:** If 1+ month since last update, prompt Jonathan to share new ratings.

### Music Philosophy
- **Through-line:** Technical mastery in service of feeling.
- **Organizing question:** Does the music have interiority?
- Heavy metal (Meshuggah, hardcore) = gym fuel, functional. Exception: Gojira (message + interiority elevate it).
- Boris defies categorization — "Vanilla" described as a physiological event.
- Deftones split: White Pony = contemplative, Around the Fur = physical.
- Medeski Martin & Wood: Scofield collab (Uberjam era) is its own register; early work strange and dark.
- Recent ceiling experience: Kasablanca at The Shop — exceptional. Seeking more of: right room + right music + right crowd + shared experience.

### Experiences Philosophy
- The experience IS the art — dissolve audience/work boundary.
- Communal energy matters (shared frequency, not just big crowd).
- Discovery over validation. Leave room for serendipity. Architecture shapes experience.
- **Ceiling experiences:** Laurie Anderson, immersive theater, Nils Frahm front row (religious experience).
- Will travel for the right experience.

- **Solo travel rule:** Refuses to visit places he'd want to share with others — preserving those for joint experiences.
- **Joint travel with Wan Ting confirmed:** CDMX, Montreal, Iceland, Tanzania (Great Migration Mara River crossing — ceiling of wildlife experience), Singapore.
