# Honeymoon 2027

## Framework
"Extreme meets bougie" — intense physical experience + luxury lodge recovery. Sossusvlei (Namibia) as the conceptual model.

## Timing
- December 12, 2026: Wedding (Casino Español, CDMX)
- February 2027: Singapore tea ceremony (Wan Ting wants this before honeymoon)
- **March 2027: Honeymoon target window**

March rationale: rhododendrons blooming (Bhutan), Patagonia late summer still excellent, comfortably first trimester if conception timing aligns, tea ceremony slots naturally into February.

---

## Leading Candidate: Bhutan

### Anchors
- **Tiger's Nest** — non-negotiable
- **Jomolhari Trek** — adventure centerpiece (opens late April/May, March timing is early but feasible)
- **Amankora circuit** — luxury through-line

### March specifics
- Rhododendrons blooming across hillsides
- Paro Tshechu festival — late March/early April, masked dance ceremonies, king participates
- Clear skies, fields green, snow still on higher peaks
- Slightly fewer tourists than October peak

### Sustainability Development Fee
Bhutan charges a daily fee — verify current rate before booking (was $200+/day as of 2023, reduced to $100/day in 2024).

---

## Second Option: Patagonia

### Anchors
- Torres del Paine — the MacGuffin
- **Explora Patagonia** — luxury lodge, guided excursions, heated pool overlooking the towers

### March specifics
- Late southern hemisphere summer
- Long days, trails in good shape
- Weather volatile — dramatic light for photography
