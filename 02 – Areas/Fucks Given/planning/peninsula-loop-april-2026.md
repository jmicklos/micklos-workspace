## Sunday, April 12 — The Payoff (mostly cloudy, 58°F)

**Route:** Lake Quinault → Hoh Rainforest → Coast → La Push → Lake Crescent → Kingston Ferry
**Google Maps:** [Sunday driving directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Lake+Quinault+Lodge,+345+S+Shore+Rd,+Quinault,+WA/Hoh+Rain+Forest+Visitor+Center,+Upper+Hoh+Rd,+Forks,+WA/Kalaloch+Tree+of+Life,+Kalaloch,+WA/Ruby+Beach,+WA/Second+Beach+Trailhead,+La+Push,+WA/Rialto+Beach,+WA/Lake+Crescent+Lodge,+Port+Angeles,+WA/Kingston+Ferry+Terminal,+Kingston,+WA)

| Time | Stop | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5:45am | **Leave Quinault** | — | North on 101, past coast (dark). East on Upper Hoh Road. 45 min. Coffee from thermos. Wan Ting eats from cooler. |
| 6:30am | **Hoh Rainforest — Hall of Mosses** | core | Sunrise ~6:35am. 0.8 mi loop. Saturday's rain hanging in canopy. Then **Spruce Nature Trail** (1.2 mi) along Hoh River — **watch for Roosevelt elk** grazing in meadows along the river at dawn. ~5,000 in the park, non-migratory, lowlands in April. 70-200mm, keep 150ft distance. |
| *~7:30am* | *Hoh River Trail (extended)* | optional | *Continue past Spruce Trail deeper into the valley. As far as you feel — the deeper you go, the fewer people. Turn around when ready.* |
| 8:30am | Drive to coast | — | Back to 101, south ~15 min. |
| 9:00am | **Kalaloch Tree of Life** | core | 2-min walk from parking. Sitka spruce suspended over eroded cave by exposed roots. Actively dying — may not survive another season. Shoot this. |
| *~9:15am* | *Kalaloch Beach 4* | optional | *Right next to Tree of Life. Wide beach, driftwood, dramatic in overcast. Less trafficked than Ruby.* |
| 9:30am | **Ruby Beach** | core | Sea stacks, black pebble, driftwood. High tide 8:53am — waves against stacks. Scan for **gray whales** offshore (April = peak migration), **harbor seals** on rock outcrops, **sea otters** in tidepools. |
| 10:15am | Drive north through Forks | — | 45 min to La Push. |
| 11:00am | **Second Beach, La Push** | core | 0.7 mi hike through forest to beach. Towering sea stacks, quieter than Ruby. The hidden one. **Bald eagles** in the treeline — look up. |
| *~11:00am* | *First Beach, La Push* | optional | *If you want a quick look without the hike — drive-up beach right in the village. Less dramatic but zero effort.* |
| 12:30pm | **Rialto Beach** | core | 15 min from La Push. Massive driftwood. Walk 1.5 mi north to Hole-in-the-Wall arch. Low tide 3:31pm — conditions improve as you go. |
| *~12:30pm* | *Third Beach* | optional | *Instead of or in addition to Rialto. More remote, fewer people, requires 1.4 mi hike. Sea stacks and waterfall on the beach.* |
| 2:00pm | Head north on 101 | — | Through Forks toward Lake Crescent. 30 min. |
| *~2:15pm* | *Forks — lunch stop* | optional | *Not a food destination but you'll need to eat. Pacific Pizza or Sully's Drive-In for quick fuel. Or eat from the cooler.* |
| 2:30pm | **Lake Crescent** | core | Glacial blue-green. South shore pullout. |
| *~3:00pm* | *Marymere Falls* | optional | *1.8 mi RT, 90-ft waterfall, easy. Trailhead at Storm King ranger station on Lake Crescent.* |
| *~3:00pm* | *Spruce Railroad Trail* | optional | *Alternative to Marymere — flat trail along north shore of Lake Crescent, through a tunnel carved into rock. 2 mi one-way to the Devil's Punchbowl.* |
| 3:30pm | Continue east | — | Lake Crescent → Port Angeles → 101 east. |
| *~4:00pm* | *Hurricane Ridge* | optional | *The big swing. 45 min up from Port Angeles. Panoramic views of Olympic range + Strait of Juan de Fuca. Only if Sunday clears — check webcam at visitor center. Adds 1.5 hrs. Push ferry to 6:30–7pm.* |
| *~4:00pm* | *Port Angeles waterfront* | optional | *If skipping Hurricane Ridge. Walk the pier, stretch, grab coffee before Kingston drive.* |
| 5:00pm | **Kingston Ferry** | core | Every ~45 min. 21-min crossing to Edmonds. Push to 6–7pm if doing Hurricane Ridge or lingering on the coast. |
| ~6:00pm | **Home** | — | |

## Gear

### Camera
- [ ] Nikon Z7 + Nikon Z7ii (both bodies)
- [ ] 24-70mm (Hoh canopy, Tree of Life, forest interiors)
- [ ] 70-200mm (sea stacks, coastal, wildlife)
- [ ] Tripod (long exposures under canopy at dawn)
- [ ] Rain cover / dry bags for both bodies — Saturday is wet, Sunday AM will be misty
- [ ] Extra batteries (cold + wet drains faster)
- [ ] Memory cards (45MP files add up across two bodies)
- [ ] Lens cloth / microfiber (mist on front element is constant in the Hoh)

### Comfort
- [ ] Blankets (1-2)
- [ ] Fold-out couch
- [ ] Rain jackets
- [ ] Layers for Sunday dawn (~50°F, damp)
- [ ] Warm fleece or puffy jacket (beach wind)
- [ ] Waterproof pants or rain shells (Hoh will be dewy)
- [ ] Hiking shoes (Second Beach + Rialto have rocky/sandy sections)
- [ ] Headlamp (5:45am departure, Hoh trail at dawn)
- [ ] Change of socks (wet feet are inevitable)
