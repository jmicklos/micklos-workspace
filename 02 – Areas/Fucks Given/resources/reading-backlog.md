# Reading Backlog

Running list of books, essays, and long-form articles to read. Tended to monthly via the "Reading backlog review" recurring responsibility in [[Fucks Given]]. Sits alongside dining, music, and travel backlogs — same Rule Zero filter applies.

## How to use
- **Queue** — add new items here as they come up. Include source if it's non-obvious (who recommended, where you saw it).
- **Currently Reading** — what's actively in progress. Cap at 2–3 to avoid parallelization tax.
- **Finished** — log with date finished + a one-line take. Helps surface patterns over time.
- **Parked** — started but set down. Not abandoned, not done.
- **Drop** — consciously decided not to finish. Useful signal.

Monthly review: move items through the stages, add new ones from whatever you've been hearing about, prune the queue if it's grown stale.
