# Experiences

## Philosophy

- **The experience IS the art.** Dissolve the audience/work boundary.
- **Communal energy matters.** Shared frequency, not just big crowd.
- **Discovery over validation.** Being the third person who knew beats the thousandth who confirmed.
- **Leave room for serendipity.** Over-scheduling kills the thing you're actually looking for. The best moments arrive sideways.
- **Architecture shapes experience.** Room matters — Kasablanca at The Shop worked because the room was right.
- **Vibrancy applies.** Same Rule Zero as dining: does someone give a fuck?
- **Will travel for the right experience.**

## Notes on Specific PNW Locations

- **Reflection Lakes / Tipsoo Lake (Rainier)** — best dawn photography spots, Rainier mirrored in still water. Tipsoo on east side via 410.
- **Nisqually NWR** — on the way south toward Rainier. Best PNW birding, boardwalk over estuary, great at dawn.
- **Federation Forest State Park** — 18 miles east of Enumclaw on 410. Old-growth Douglas fir, White River. Good April stop en route to Rainier. Verify 410 road conditions (flood damage Dec 2025 near milepost 38).
- **Carkeek Park** — underrated Seattle beach/bluff, railroad tracks along waterline, good for shadow-forward photography.
- **Montlake Fill (Union Bay Natural Area)** — best urban birding in Seattle, peak migration in April.
