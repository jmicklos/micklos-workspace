# Photography

## Gear

- **Bodies:** Nikon Z7 + Nikon Z7ii (dual-body setup — "combo breaker" approach)
- **Lenses:** 24-70mm on one body, 70-200mm on the other. Avoids lens swapping in the field.
- **Note:** 45MP on both — no resolution trade-off when switching reach. Exceptional shadow recovery headroom in RAW. A dedicated wide prime may be missing (gap below 24mm felt on landscape/architecture work).
- **Workflow:** Field -> import -> Lightroom edit. Not tethered. RAW exclusively.
- **Post approach:** Global adjustments + local masking. "Art >> purity." Shadow-forward tonal intention extended in post, not correction of mistakes.

---

## Style

- Dark, atmospheric, underexposed by choice — shoots toward shadow
- Subject isn't going anywhere in ghost town work -> rewards patience and stillness (different muscle than wildlife/street)
- Range: wildlife, street, landscape, abandonment/entropy, macro, architecture, night sky
- Portraiture: close, unguarded, emotionally direct — catches people not-noticing
- Philosophy: "The perfect shot can't be the enemy of the good shot" — dual-body setup reduces friction between intention and capture

---

## Core Discipline

"Why is this beautiful? How is this evocative?" — puts Jonathan in a good headspace. Photography as an active practice, not passive documentation.

Namibia sand-filled interiors (Kolmanskop-style) mirrors ghost town sensibility — a complete world abandoned whole.
