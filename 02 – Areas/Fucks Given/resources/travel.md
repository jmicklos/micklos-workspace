# Travel

## Frameworks

### Destination Experience Model

Fixed endpoint. Logistics in service of it. The trip is a delivery mechanism. Optimize to minimize friction between you and the thing. You identify the thing — Joe Beef, Bhutan's Tiger's Nest, Willows Inn — and everything else follows.

### MacGuffin Model

The object at the end almost doesn't matter — the pretext gives the trip a spine and direction. Frame makes space for serendipity. Maximize interesting surface area along the route. Without the MacGuffin you're aimlessly driving; with it you're on a mission and everything unexpected is a bonus. (Example: Mojave ghost town cluster — Ludlow is not the point, the Route 66 drive through the Mojave is.)

**Both require an anchor.** Anchorless travel is noise. The distinction is whether the anchor demands you show up (destination) or gives you permission to go looking (MacGuffin).

## Solo Travel Rule

Jonathan refuses to visit places he'd want to share with others — preserving those for joint experiences. Solo travel skews toward harder, weirder, more physically demanding destinations. Ghost town drives, abandoned industrial sites, places that require tolerance for discomfort or obscurity.

## Honeymoon Planning

**Framework:** "Extreme meets bougie" — intense physical experience + luxury lodge recovery. Sossusvlei as the model.

**Target:** March 2027 (post-wedding December 2026 -> Singapore tea ceremony flexible -> honeymoon early conception window)

### Bhutan (Leading Candidate)
- **Tiger's Nest** (Paro Taktsang) — non-negotiable anchor. 3-4 hour hike to monastery clinging to cliff face.
- **Jomolhari Trek** — 8-11 days, base of Jomolhari peak, remote high-altitude wilderness.
- **Amankora circuit** — 5 lodges across country (Paro, Thimphu, Punakha, Gangtey, Bumthang). Luxury through-line.
- **March timing:** Pre-monsoon. Rhododendrons blooming, clear mountain views.
- **Logistics:** Sustainable Development Fee ($100/person/day). Visa required. All tourism must be through licensed operator.

### Patagonia (Second Option)
- Torres del Paine National Park, Chile.
- Anchored at Explora Patagonia lodge.
- W Trek (4-5 days) or full O Circuit (9 days) as physical centerpiece.
- Milodon Cave, Grey Glacier, Los Cuernos.
- March timing: Late summer in southern hemisphere, good weather, fewer crowds than January/February.
