# Packing Lists

Master packing reference for all trip types: roadtrips, camping, overland adventure, backpacking, flying somewhere, etc. Use this as a pick-list — not everything applies to every trip.

## How to use

- **Pick by trip type**: scan categories, pull relevant items
- **"How Packing" column** tells you where the item lives day-to-day:
  - **Go Case** — pre-packed grab-and-go kit
  - **4Runner / 4Runner Case** — lives in the vehicle
  - **Dopp Kit** — toiletry bag, always packed
  - **From Home** — grab from house before departure
  - **Buy On Road** — purchase en route
