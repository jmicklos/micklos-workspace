### Freezer-Door Cocktails (Boat Flight — 2026-06-13)

Built neat into ~8.5 oz swing-top bottles, frozen, served over ice. Spirit-forward / stirred only — no fresh citrus needed. Over ice, build neat and fill the bottle; the ice dilutes as you sip (~20–25% by the bottom of the glass). No-ice service: build at ~6.5 oz spirit + 1.3 oz water instead.

#### Black Manhattan — QUEUE
- 5.5 oz whiskey, 2.5 oz amaro (Averna recommended), 3 dashes Angostura + 1 dash orange bitters
- Manhattan variant; amaro replaces sweet vermouth. Darker, bitter-edged.
- Amaro wants medium-bodied, caramel/cola: Averna (canonical), Montenegro (brighter), Nonino (drier). Avoid Fernet at full pour — bulldozes the whiskey.
- **Boat verdict: hit.** Queue for freezer rotation.

#### Boulevardier
- 4 oz whiskey, 2 oz Campari, 2 oz sweet vermouth
- Equal-parts 2:1:1. "Negroni but whiskey."

#### Brooklyn (Amargo de Chile riff)
- 5 oz rye, 2 oz dry vermouth, 0.5 oz Amer Picon, 0.5 oz Amargo de Chile, 3 dashes Angostura
- Amargo de Chile (CH Distillery — mole/chile/cocoa) stands in for maraschino. Reads dark, spiced, cocoa-forward — closer to a mole-Manhattan than a textbook Brooklyn.
- Luxardo Maraschino turns this into the real Brooklyn.

#### Negroni
- 2.75 oz gin (workhorse), 2.75 oz Campari, 2.5 oz sweet vermouth
- Use workhorse gin, not the good stuff — Campari talks over delicate botanicals.

#### White Negroni (Malört)
- 3 oz gin (workhorse), 3 oz Lillet Blanc, 2 oz Malört
- Malört as wormwood stand-in for Suze/gentian. Pale + bitter. Suze makes the textbook version.

#### Martini (Brass Lion Singapore Dry)
- 5.5 oz Brass Lion gin, 2.5 oz dry vermouth (Dolin-style), no bitters
- Showcase bottle: torch ginger, lemongrass, chrysanthemum. Run moderately wet on purpose — bone-dry mutes delicate botanicals. Lemon twist, never olive.

#### El Capitán
- 5.5 oz pisco, 2.5 oz sweet vermouth, 4 dashes Angostura
- Peruvian Manhattan. The stirred pisco classic — solves "what to do with pisco without citrus."

### Freezer-Door Heuristics
- Freezer-door = stirred, spirit-forward, no fresh citrus. Sours need same-day citrus.
- Over ice → build neat, fill the bottle. No ice → leave room for ~1/5 water.
- Bitters complexity should be *inverse* to the amaro's complexity.
- Spend gift/showcase spirits where they're tasted naked (Martini), not where a loud modifier buries them (Negroni).
- Aromatic gin: moderately wet beats bone-dry. Lemon twist over olive.
