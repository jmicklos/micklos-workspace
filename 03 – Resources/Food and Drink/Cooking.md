## Cast Iron Steak — Filet Mignon

**Proven method** — 2026-06-10, medium rare, nailed it.

### Setup
- **Cut:** 1.5" filet mignon
- **Fat:** Beef tallow (smoke point ~400°F)
- **Pan:** Cast iron, preheated on high ~5 min

### Prep
- Pull steaks from fridge 45 min before cooking
- Pat bone dry, salt generously on all sides

### Cook
- High heat throughout
- **3.5 min** top (first side down)
- **3.5 min** bottom (flip once)
- **20–30 sec** per edge (use tongs to hold upright)

### Rest
- Rest 8–10 min loosely tented with foil

### Sides that worked
- Wild rice blend
- Sauteed bok choy with carrots
- Red wine

### Notes
- Tallow gave a great deep crust with no gray band — wall-to-wall pink
- No butter baste needed; the tallow carried enough flavor
- Trust the thermometer over the clock — pull at 125–128°F for carryover to ~130–133°F

## Bitter Melon & Egg Stir-Fry (苦瓜炒蛋)

**Proven method** — 2026-07-31, delicious. Cantonese/Singaporean home stir-fry. Great foil to a rich seared steak; pairs with leftover rice. Serves 2 as a side.

### Ingredients
- 1 medium bitter melon
- 3 eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt + white pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (fermented black beans / douchi optional — great here)
- 1 tsp sugar (balances the bitter)
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- ~2 tbsp neutral oil
- Salt for the pre-treat

### Prep — key step for taming bitterness
- Halve lengthwise. Scoop out seeds **and scrape away the white spongy pith** (most bitter part).
- Slice thin on a slight angle (~1/4").
- Toss with ~1 tsp salt, rest **15–20 min** (it weeps water). Rinse well and squeeze/pat dry.
- Optional: a 30-sec blanch tames it further for a milder result.

### Cook
- Hot pan/wok, oil, garlic (+ black beans) ~20 sec until fragrant.
- Add bitter melon, stir-fry 2–3 min until crisp-tender.
- Add sugar + soy, toss.
- Push melon aside, pour egg into the space, let it set ~10 sec, then fold everything together until egg is just cooked and coats the melon. Soft scramble — don't overcook.
- Taste, adjust salt. Serve over rice.

### Notes
- Dial bitterness: more salt-rest + a blanch = milder; skip blanch and go light on sugar = keep it assertive
- Scraping the white pith is the single biggest lever on bitterness
