## Research Notes & Decisions

- **2UZ-FE is an interference engine** — belt failure = catastrophic piston-to-valve damage. Do not delay.
- **Kit trap:** TKT-025 = V6, TKT-001 = V8 but no hydraulic tensioner. Must use **TKT-021** only.
- **Thermostat trap:** 90916-03075 fits V6/3.4L. Must use **90916-03100** for V8.
- **Camshaft seals:** Some shops suggest inspect-only. Insist on replacement — history of seal failure causing alternator damage at 115k.
- **5th gen rear caliper swap** — not a meaningful upgrade. Rears replaced Jan 2024 (~21k miles ago), not worth doing now.
- **Bigger brake kits (Wilwood, Powerbrake)** — evaluated and deferred. Not warranted for current build.
- **Akebono ACT976** confirmed correct pad for 5th gen caliper/rotor spec.
- **Mishimoto MMRAD-4RUN-03** — ideal to do during timing belt job since cooling system is already drained.
- **Fan clutch cold roar** — normal on 2UZ-FE. Cold startup roar quieting after warmup is not a fault. Replace anyway at 174k during timing belt job.
- **5th gen caliper fitment:** 2010–2014 spec only (14WA casting, 12mm bolt). 2015+ (14WB, 14mm) will not fit 4th gen knuckles.
