## Research Notes

### Korean Bronze Tableware (Bangjja Yugi)

Featured on Business Insider's "So Expensive" series. Bangjja yugi is hand-forged Korean bronzeware (78% copper, 22% tin) historically used by Korean royalty. Designated an Important Intangible Cultural Property by South Korea in 1983. Properties: natural antibacterial sterilization, exceptional heat retention (superior to porcelain and stainless steel), microwave safe, and can detect pesticide residue. Each piece is hand-forged by trained artisans.
