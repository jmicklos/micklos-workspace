# Home – Remodel – Bedroom Reading Lights

## Definition of Done
Two reading lights installed on either side of the bed in the primary bedroom, with dimming control via a wireless remote or smart bulb.

## Context
The primary bedroom has hardwired junction boxes on either side of the bed for reading lights. No built-in switch at the fixture location — control needs to come from either a smart relay, a smart bulb, or a fixture with a built-in switch.

## What We Bought

**Control approach chosen: Option C — fixture with built-in switch.** Solved the switch problem by combining a spotlight with an add-on switch back plate from the same shop.

### Control Options Considered

**Option A: Zigbee Dimmer Relay + Wireless Remote** — Decouple switch from fixture. Best pairing: Sunricher ZG2835 relay + ZG2836 rotary remote (~$55-60/light). Budget: Moes dimmer + IKEA RODRET (~$30/light).

**Option B: Smart Bulb** — Simplest install, but power loss state issues.

**Option C: Fixture with Built-in Switch** — Originally seemed too limited/expensive, but the SmileLampWorks add-on switch solved this.
