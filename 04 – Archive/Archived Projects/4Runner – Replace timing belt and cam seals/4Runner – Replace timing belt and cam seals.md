## Project 2: Front Brake Upgrade (5th Gen Swap)

**Background:** Recurring warped rotors due to 4th gen caliper design — notorious for sticky pistons causing uneven heat and wear. Fix addresses root cause by swapping to 5th gen calipers + matched rotors: larger diameter (319mm → 338mm), thicker rotors (28mm → 31.8mm), larger pistons (45.3mm → 48mm). Limited trim runs stock 18" wheels — **clears the 5th gen hardware with no fitment issues.**

**Caliper fitment note:** Use **2010–2014** 5th gen spec (14WA casting, 12mm mounting bolt). 2015+ units use 14mm mounting hardware (14WB) and are **not compatible** with 4th gen knuckles.

**Expected labor:** 1.5–2 hours **Target shop cost (labor only):** $200–$320 **All-in target (parts + labor):** $550–$750
