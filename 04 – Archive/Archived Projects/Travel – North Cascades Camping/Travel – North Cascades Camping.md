# Travel – North Cascades Camping

## Overview

- **Dates:** Fri Jun 19 – Sun Jun 21, 2026
- **Check-in:** After 2:00 PM | **Check-out:** Before 11:00 AM
- **Campsite:** High Ridge Retreat – Site 1 (Hipcamp booking #4136642)
- **Location:** 48°30'56.6"N 121°21'59.1"W (North Cascades, near Marblemount)
- **Google Maps:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=48.515708931922234,-121.36641999669494&z=13

- **Group:** 4 adults / 2 vehicles — Jonathan, Wan Ting, Yuani Chen, dlim08@gmail.com
- **Camping type:** Tent
- **Host:** Alexandrea De Coria — +13604416515

## Site Rules & Info

- **No potable water** — bring your own
- **Campfires allowed** (firepit, communal) — check info board for burn bans
- **Toilets:** Flush/pit toilets at main bathroom, ~10 min drive. Right off Bernhard Bridge at 4-way intersection, restrooms on left. Bring flashlight (park not well lit)
- **No shower**
- **Pets allowed** (on leash)
- **Trash bin on site** — take trash with you on departure, dumpster at campground exit
- **No kitchen/cooking equipment provided**
- **Quiet time:** 10pm–7am
- **No fireworks**
- **Speed limit:** 15 mph in park
- **Visitors must sign in** at guest book

## Directions (from booking)

1. Lock boxes at front gate guest sign — enter code to get key card
2. Only 1 vehicle per card scan (scan again for 2nd vehicle)
3. Return key card to lock box at check-out
4. After gate: info board straight ahead (burn ban / water restriction updates)
5. Turn **right** at info board, down the hill, go straight over the bridge
6. **Immediately after bridge, turn right** (only option)
7. At the fork, **keep left**
8. Follow road to Diablo/High Ridge Rd — **turn left** onto High Ridge Dr
9. Property is up the hill on your right — white sign "High Ridge Retreat"
