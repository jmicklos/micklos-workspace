# Life's Work – Cascades Satellite Wi-Fi Advocacy

## Goal
Get rail-certified satellite connectivity procured for Amtrak Cascades, ideally integrated into the incoming Siemens Airo trainsets before they enter revenue service.

## Done state
One of:
- WSDOT commits to a satellite connectivity pilot on Cascades with a timeline
- Sen. Pedersen + Sen. Liias land a funding line for connectivity in the 2027–2029 biennial budget
- WSDOT provides a written justification closing the door on satellite integration during the Airo delivery window

## Strategic timing
The Airo delivery is the cheap integration window. Retrofitting rail-certified satellite terminals (Icomera, Clarus, and similar EN50155-compliant vendors) before Airos enter revenue service is materially less expensive than post-delivery retrofit. Once the Airos are in service without satellite hardware, the current Nomad Digital cellular-aggregation architecture effectively locks in for another equipment generation.

## Key facts
- Current architecture: Nomad Digital cellular aggregation, installed on Cascades January 2014
- Proven precedent: Brightline (FL) running Starlink for rail since May 2023
- FCC approved Starlink on moving vehicles: June 30, 2022
- Vehicle penetration loss in rail carriages: up to 28 dB (UK DfT study)
- Corridor-specific issue: BNSF ROW diverges from I-5 through Lewis/Cowlitz counties where cellular tower density drops

### Airo trainset program status
- 8 Cascades Airo trainsets on order (of 83 total Airo units across all Amtrak routes)
- **3 manufactured as of 2026-04-21** (third unit heading to Northeast Corridor for testing)
- **5 still in production** — these are the remaining factory-integration window
- Entry into service: "later this year" per Amtrak (2026)
- Source: https://media.amtrak.com/2026/04/the-third-new-amtrak-cascades-trainset-is-on-the-move-%F0%9F%9A%86/
