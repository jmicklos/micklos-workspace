# Home – Living Room Furniture

## Goal
Furnish the main floor living room with a floating furniture arrangement centered on the fireplace.

## Room Constraints

- **Dimensions:** 19'5" (E–W) x 15' (N–S)
- **Usable furniture zone:** ~11'8" deep x 15' wide (after 30" hearth + 63" walkway)
- **Fireplace:** Centered on south wall, hearth projects 30" into room
- **Minisplit:** Floor unit on south wall, west of fireplace
- **Big window:** West wall
- **Entrances:** Hallway (63", east wall NE corner), Study (6', east wall SE corner), Foyer (63", west wall NW corner)
- **Furniture floats** in center with walkable space on all sides (~30" min)
- **East wall:** Keep clear for future credenza/sideboard

## Furniture Plan

### Layout: D2 — L-shape opening toward big window
Two separate cloud-style sofas positioned perpendicular to form an L:
- **Long sofa** parallel to fireplace (facing it)
- **Shorter sofa/loveseat** perpendicular, running toward the big window (west wall)
- L opens toward natural light from the big window

### Key Considerations
- **Budget:** $3,000–$6,000 for both sofas combined
- **Style:** Bulbous, puffy, marshmallow aesthetic (Wan Ting's preference). NOT angular/mid-century.
- **Performance fabric** required (stain-resistant, durable)
- **Cushions must last** long-term and/or be replaceable
- Cloud couches are deep (38-46") — budget accordingly in the 11'8" usable depth
- Don't block minisplit airflow on south wall
- Don't block walkway between hallway and foyer entrances (north side, 63" zone)
- Tudor Revival aesthetic — consider whether furniture complements or intentionally contrasts

## Sofa Research

### Style Direction (2026-04-15, updated 2026-04-19)
Target aesthetic: **bulbous, marshmallow-shaped sofas with taut upholstery** — rounded, segmented cushion forms where the entire silhouette is puffy. Smooth fabric stretched tight over bulging forms, NOT slipcovered/wrinkly. No visible frame or hard edges.

**Closest reference (2026-04-19):** [Casa Dio Marshmallow Sofa](https://casadiousa.com/products/marshmallow-sofa) — shape is right, but:
- Price (~$13k) is way over budget
- Needs **higher back / lower arms** so there are actual arms (Casa Dio version has no real arms)

### Dimension Fit Check
With 11'8" (140") of usable depth and 30" walkways on each side of floating furniture:
- Max sofa depth: ~80" (140" minus 30" walkway on each side) — ALL options fit on depth
- Max sofa width (long piece, parallel to fireplace): ~9' (15' minus 30" walkway each side = 120") — all options fit
- The perpendicular piece (toward window) is more constrained at ~80" max width
