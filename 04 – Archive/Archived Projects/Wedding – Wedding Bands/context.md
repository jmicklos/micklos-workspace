## Key decisions & rationale
- **14K yellow gold over 18K**: More durable for daily wear and meaningfully cheaper. 18K is richer color but not worth the premium for a plain band.
- **Blue Nile as primary retailer**: Best pricing by $100-225+ vs Brilliant Earth for equivalent plain bands. Classic 14K YG dome: $981 (6mm), $1,125 (7mm), ~$1,300 est (8mm).
- **Brilliant Earth as fallback**: 10-20% more expensive but confirmed quarter sizing (8.75 available). Relevant if Blue Nile can't do 8.75 and Jonathan picks 6mm.
- **Portland purchase**: Both Blue Nile (Washington Square, Tigard) and Brilliant Earth (NW Glisan) have Portland showrooms. Oregon has 0% sales tax vs ~10.25% in Seattle — saves $100-133 on a $1,000-1,300 band.
- **Comfort fit not required**: Jonathan said standard fit is fine, saves $40-100.
- **Brand is irrelevant**: Jonathan's view — plain gold band is a commodity, optimize for price and sizing.
- **Frank Darling ruled out**: "Dad Band" maxes at 5.5mm, too narrow for what Jonathan wants.
- **7mm added as an option**: Wasn't originally considered but makes sense as a middle ground between 6mm and 8mm.
