## 2026-06-12 (cont.) — Heft added as a priority; metal decision → 18K

### What Jonathan Said
- "while I want a good design, I also want something with a lot of heft. how can I make sure of that before buying online?"
- "let's go with 18k"

### Decisions
- **Material decided: 18K yellow gold** (reverses earlier 14K lean). Driver = heft now a stated priority; 18K is 75% gold vs 14K's 58%, so denser/heavier + richer color. Tradeoff accepted: 18K softer/pricier.
- **Heft is now an explicit buying criterion**, verifiable before purchase via **finished gram weight in his size**: ~6–9g light, 10–14g solid, **15g+ = DY-tier heft**. Target 15g+.
- **Standard/flat fit, NOT comfort fit** — comfort fit hollows interior and removes grams. Reinforces "comfort fit not required."
- Tall profile + heft goals reinforce each other (raised crown = more metal). Gram weight doubles as a proxy check that the profile is genuinely tall, not flat.

### How-to-verify-heft-online (captured in note)
Ask seller for finished gram weight in size; reputable sellers provide it (refusal = yellow flag). Compare apples-to-apples (same size/metal). Buy-and-try: weigh on kitchen scale, free return if light. Custom: specify target gram weight / "solid, no hollowing."
