## Key decisions & rationale
- **Frame TV chosen** because Jonathan already has one in the basement and knows the One Invisible Connection cable is thin enough to route along the white fireplace surround.
- **One Connect box at floor level** to one side of the fireplace — all HDMI sources plug in there, keeping cables away from the alcove.
- **No Smurf tube** exists from the alcove to either side — this is the core constraint. Alcove has power + Ethernet only. Any cable between the alcove and floor level must run externally along the surround.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Sonos Port/optical (~75ms latency), AirPlay (latency + single-input), in-ceiling speakers (too much construction), media console below (fireplace goes to floor), off-center soundbar placement (Jonathan explicitly against), soundbar mounted to TV (Samsung doesn't support it), Leon FrameBar (~$4k, still needs wired connection).
- **Q-Symphony is Samsung-only** — no third-party soundbar can wirelessly connect to a Samsung TV. This was confirmed via research.
