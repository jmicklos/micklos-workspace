# Worklog — Home – Mount Dining Room Light

## 2026-06-12 — Initial setup and mounting question

### Situation
- Jonathan is installing a new light fixture in the dining room
- The ceiling currently has a single round junction box with a bare bulb sticking out
- The new fixture has a rectangular brass/bronze backplate with two silver mounting brackets on each end and a green ground wire
- The mismatch between the single junction box and the two-point fixture mount was confusing

### Approach & Rationale
- This is a standard mismatch solved by a crossbar/mounting strap bracket
- The crossbar screws into the two threaded holes inside the junction box (every standard box has them)
- The fixture's two mounting brackets then bolt to the ends of the crossbar
- Most fixtures include a crossbar in the hardware bag — check there first

### What Jonathan Said
- "How am I supposed to attach the bottom of this light mount to the ceiling when the light mount has two mount points and the ceiling has only one"
- "This feels like it should be a common problem" — it is, extremely common
