### 🪵 STEP 1 — Site Preparation (do first — most important)

## Scope

- Remove weeds/grass from parking strip and front woodland areas
- Remove invasive roots
- Light grading if needed
- Loosen top 4–6" soil

## Soil improvement

- Add 2–3" compost
- Till or mix into soil

## Woodland base

- Spread 2–3" arborist wood chips across front yard beds
- Keep 3–4" away from tree trunks

**Result:** woodland soil ecosystem + weed suppression.

---

### 🌿 STEP 2 — Install Parking Strip (Permanent)

#### Base layer — Pachysandra ‘Green Carpet’

##### Quantity

- ~400–450 plants total

##### Layout

- 8–10" staggered spacing
- Continuous carpet
- Avoid planting within 18" of trees

##### Planting method

1. Lay plants on grid first
2. Dig hole slightly larger than plug
3. Plant level with soil
4. Water deeply

---

#### Accent layer — Fern rhythm

##### Placement

- 3–4 ferns total
- 1 every ~10–12 ft
- Offset from center (natural placement)

##### Species

- Sword fern → bold
- Deer fern → refined

---

#### Finish

- Apply 2" bark mulch between plants
- Water thoroughly

---

### 🏺 STEP 3 — Install Terracotta Urn Placeholders (Future Fence Line)

#### Purpose

- Establish visual boundary where brick/wrought iron fence will go
- Create Tudor estate structure now
- Avoid permanent planting in construction zone

---

#### Recommended style

##### Size

- 18–24" diameter minimum
- 20–36" tall ideal

##### Quantity

- 2 (minimum) — at stair top
- Optional 2 more to suggest fence endpoints

---

#### Placement

Plain text

\[URN\]   stair landing   \[URN\]

or along future fence alignment.

---

#### Installation method

- Set on compacted gravel base (not soil)
- Ensure level
- Allow drainage hole
