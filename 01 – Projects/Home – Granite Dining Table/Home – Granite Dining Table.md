## Definition of Done
Black granite slab is cut, mounted on a table base, and installed in the dining room.

## Context
- Have a large black granite slab (uncut, 1.25" thick) to use as a dining room table top
- Granite will be cut larger than the base — overhang creates the final table size
- Granite adds 1.25" of height and ~400-500 lbs of weight
- Sydney base is built for stone tops (travertine) — no additional reinforcement needed

## Notes
- Standard dining table height is 28-30". Viggo is likely 30". With 1.25" granite = ~31.25" (fine, or trim base 1").
- Overhang rules (Marble Institute): 1.25" thick granite = 10" max unsupported overhang. 1/3 rule also applies (overhang can't exceed 1/3 of total slab depth).
- Table cantilever is different from countertop support — confirm with fabricator. 8" overhang is conservative, 10" is the theoretical max.
- At 100" (8" overhang each end): seats 8 comfortably. At 104" (10" overhang): seats 8-10.
