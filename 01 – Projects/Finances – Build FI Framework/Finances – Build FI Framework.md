### The staged plan — "all of the above, staged"
Three targets, different math, sequenced:
1. **F-you money** — near-term *liquidity* target. Mostly taxable/accessible assets. What lets you quit.
2. **Coast FIRE** — invest aggressively now so compounding alone reaches retirement even with zero future contributions.
3. **Full FIRE** — long-horizon *asset* target. Work fully optional.

### The tax nuance (the key insight)
"Assets × 25" (4% rule) is **not tax-blind but glosses over account location**, which changes the real number:
- **Roth** — withdrawals tax-free. $1 = $1 spendable. Most efficient dollar.
- **Traditional 401k/IRA** — withdrawals taxed as ordinary income. Need *more* to net the same spend.
- **Taxable brokerage** — growth taxed at long-term cap gains (0/15/20%). In between.

**The honest method:** define target *after-tax spendable income* → work backward through the account mix to find the pre-tax assets required. This is the "what does comfortable actually mean" work — put real numbers on each stage.
