### The tax teaching (for a cold reader)
Why "assets × 25" is incomplete: a dollar in a Roth (tax-free withdrawal) is worth more than a dollar in a 401k (taxed as income) or taxable brokerage (cap gains). Two people with the same Number have different real independence depending on account mix. So targets must be defined in after-tax spendable terms.
