Councilmember Hollingsworth,

I'm writing as a Capitol Hill homeowner to request your office's direct intervention on a recurring encampment at 1102 19th Ave E that the city's reporting system has failed to resolve over three months.

Since March 2026, I have filed four separate Find It Fix It reports for this location (#26-00084320, #26-00089847, #26-00131378, #26-00158349). My fiancée has filed two additional reports. In total, six reports from two residents at the same address. Every single report except my latest has been marked as "may have resolved itself."

It has not. The UCT closes these reports based on daytime verification photos, but this is a nightly problem. A daytime check will never capture it. This process cannot produce a resolution — only closed tickets.

The situation on our block is escalating. The recurring encampment has attracted other homeless individuals to the neighborhood. My fiancée no longer feels safe walking at night in a neighborhood we moved to specifically for that sense of safety. As early as December 2025 I reported an abandoned RV on this block (#25-00398473) with loose propane canisters sitting in the street — a clear safety hazard. For whatever reason, this report was also removed from my Find It Fix It app. Thankfully the abandoned RV situation has actually resolved itself.

Since Grants Pass v. Johnson, the city has every legal tool available to enforce its anti-camping ordinances. I would prefer the compassionate option first — connect this individual with shelter and outreach services. REACH operates in Capitol Hill. The CARE Department exists for this kind of situation. To my knowledge, neither has been deployed to this address despite three months of reports. But if outreach does not resolve this, I am asking the city to enforce. Three months of inaction is enough.

Since 2020, I have been investing in a full studs-out remodel of a 1930s Tudor Revival home on this block (new sewer junction at the main, asbestos/oil tank remediation, complete new water/gas/electric ... the works). My fiancée and I moved in last year. This is a significant personal and financial investment in this community. I am entitled to transparency on how the city is handling this situation. I have repeatedly flagged the pattern of reports being closed without resolution — on the app itself and now through this email — and so far have been shut down every time. Being told the issue "may have resolved itself" five times in three months is not governance. It is a system designed to make complaints disappear.

I am separately filing a formal complaint with the Seattle Customer Service Bureau disputing the pattern of report closures, and I am submitting this same concern to the Mayor's office.

I am willing to help in any capacity I can to get this resolved. I would appreciate a response from your office with a specific plan of action for this address — not another closed ticket.
