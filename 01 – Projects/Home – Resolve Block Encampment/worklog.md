# Worklog — Home – Resolve Block Encampment

## 2026-06-07 — Project kickoff

### Situation
- A homeless individual has been sleeping under an awning on Jonathan's block in Capitol Hill nightly
- He leaves during the day but returns each night
- Jonathan has been using the Seattle "Find It Fix It" app to report it, but it has done nothing
- Jonathan wants to know what else he can do

### Approach & Rationale
- Lead with outreach services (REACH, Seattle Human Services) rather than enforcement, because:
  - *Martin v. Boise* limits Seattle's ability to enforce against sleeping in public when shelter isn't available
  - Find It Fix It is essentially toothless for this situation
  - REACH specifically operates in Capitol Hill and is the city's actual mechanism for this
- Practical deterrents (lights, sprinklers) as a secondary track if outreach doesn't work
- Neighbor coordination and council rep contact for collective pressure

### What Jonathan Said
- Find It Fix It "seems to do nothing about it"
- "He leaves and comes back night after night"
- Wants actionable next steps beyond the app
