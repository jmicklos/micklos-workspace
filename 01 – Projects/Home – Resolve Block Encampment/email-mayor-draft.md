**Subject:** Recurring Nightly Encampment at 1102 19th Ave E — Find It Fix It Failing

Mayor Wilson,

I'm writing to bring a recurring encampment at 1102 19th Ave E in Capitol Hill to your attention. The city's Find It Fix It reporting system has failed to resolve this issue despite six reports from two residents over three months.

Since March 2026, I have filed four encampment reports for this location (#26-00084320, #26-00089847, #26-00131378, #26-00158349). My fiancée has filed two additional reports. Every closed report was marked as "may have resolved itself." The individual returns most nights and has done so since well before my first report.

The Unified Care Team closes these reports based on daytime verification photos for a problem that only occurs at night. This process cannot produce a resolution — only closed tickets.

This is exactly the kind of situation your CARE Department was created for — a person who needs to be connected with shelter and outreach services, not enforcement. REACH operates in Capitol Hill. To my knowledge, neither CARE nor REACH has been deployed to this address. I would prefer the compassionate option first, but if outreach does not resolve this, I am asking the city to enforce. Since Grants Pass v. Johnson, the city has every tool available to do so. Three months of inaction is enough.

The situation on our block is escalating. The encampment has attracted other homeless individuals to the neighborhood. My fiancée no longer feels safe walking at night in a neighborhood we moved to specifically for that sense of safety. Since 2020, I have been investing in a full studs-out remodel of a 1930s Tudor Revival home on this block (new sewer junction at the main, asbestos/oil tank remediation, complete new water/gas/electric ... the works). My fiancée and I moved in last year. This is a significant personal and financial investment in this community, and I am entitled to transparency on how the city is handling this situation.

I am also escalating this to Councilmember Hollingsworth's office and filing a formal complaint with the Customer Service Bureau.

I am willing to help in any capacity I can to get this resolved.

Respectfully,
