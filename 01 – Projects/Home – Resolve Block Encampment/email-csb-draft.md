**Subject:** Formal Dispute — Repeated Closure of Encampment Reports at 1102 19th Ave E

Ms. Harmston, Ms. Grove, and leadership at the Customer Service Bureau,

I am writing to formally dispute the repeated closure of service requests for a recurring unauthorized encampment at 1102 19th Ave E in Capitol Hill.

I have filed four encampment reports at this address since March 2026: #26-00084320, #26-00089847, #26-00131378, and #26-00158349. My fiancée has filed two additional reports. Every closed report was marked as "may have resolved itself." The problem has not resolved itself.

The individual returns to this location most nights and has done so since well before my first report. The closure pattern indicates that the Unified Care Team conducts daytime inspections, finds no one present, and closes the report. This is a nightly encampment. Inspecting during the day and closing based on daytime conditions is a process failure — it is structurally incapable of capturing a recurring nightly problem.

The situation on our block is escalating. The recurring encampment has attracted other homeless individuals to the neighborhood. My fiancée no longer feels safe walking at night in a neighborhood we moved to specifically for that sense of safety. I have also had a report removed from my Find It Fix It app entirely (#25-00398473, an abandoned RV on the same block with loose propane canisters in the street). Reports should not be disappearing from the app without explanation.

Since 2020, I have been investing in a full studs-out remodel of a 1930s Tudor Revival home on this block (new sewer junction at the main, asbestos/oil tank remediation, complete new water/gas/electric ... the works). My fiancée and I moved in last year. This is a significant personal and financial investment in this community.

I am requesting:

1. That the closures of reports #26-00084320, #26-00089847, and #26-00131378 be reviewed and the closure rationale formally documented
2. That the current open report (#26-00158349) not be closed until the recurring nightly encampment has actually been resolved — not merely unoccupied at the time of a daytime inspection
3. That the UCT conduct at least one inspection during evening or nighttime hours to observe the actual conditions at this site
4. An explanation of what process exists for recurring encampments that are only present at night, since the current process clearly cannot address them

I have also escalated this matter to Councilmember Hollingsworth's office and the Mayor's office.
