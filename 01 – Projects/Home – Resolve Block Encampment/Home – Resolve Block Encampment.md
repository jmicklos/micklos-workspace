# Home — Resolve Block Encampment

## Goal
Get the recurring encampment under the awning on our block resolved through city outreach and/or practical deterrents. Find It Fix It has been ineffective.

## Context
- A homeless individual sleeps under an awning on our block nightly
- He leaves during the day but returns each night
- Find It Fix It reports have produced no results — 6 reports filed over 3+ months, some deleted by the city
- Per *Martin v. Boise*, Seattle can't enforce against sleeping in public when shelter is unavailable — so enforcement isn't a realistic path
- Capitol Hill, Seattle (District 3)
