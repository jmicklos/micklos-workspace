# Home – Backyard Cleanup

Prerequisite to [[Home – V1 Landscaping]] rear yard work. The backyard is overgrown with invasive plants, littered with construction debris from the remodel, and generally unusable. Goal: clear it out to a blank slate so landscaping can begin.

## Definition of Done
- All construction debris removed (lumber scraps, cinder blocks, old door/panel, bucket, misc)
- Invasive plants identified and removed (bittersweet nightshade confirmed, likely others)
- Fig tree preserved and cleared around
- Rose bushes preserved and cleared around (pale pink, likely from previous owner — transplant in Nov if needed)
- Overgrown hedge/vegetation cut back to manageable state
- Temporary shed area tidied
- Yard is a clean canvas for V1 Landscaping rear phase

## Keep
- **Fig tree** — in the overgrown mass along the left side. Identify, flag, and protect before clearing.
- **Rose bushes** — pale pink, surviving from previous owner pre-construction. Still blooming. Clear around and protect. If they need to move for landscaping, transplant in **November** (dormant season) — do NOT transplant in summer.

## Remove
- **Bittersweet nightshade** (Solanum dulcamara) — invasive, mildly toxic. Purple flowers, red berries. Pull by roots; bag and trash (do NOT compost). Wear gloves.
- **Other invasive growth** — ID anything else in the overgrown mass before pulling. Likely blackberry, bindweed, or English ivy mixed in.
- **Construction debris** — cinder blocks, lumber, old door/panel, bucket, cardboard, misc materials visible in photo
- **General overgrowth** — cut back the hedge wall and tangled vegetation to expose the actual yard footprint
