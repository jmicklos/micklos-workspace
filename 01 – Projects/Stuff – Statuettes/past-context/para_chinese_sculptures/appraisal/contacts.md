## Appraisal Types Needed

1. **Auction estimate** (free, no obligation) — start here with Bonhams
2. **Insurance appraisal** (formal written document, fee-based) — needed for coverage
3. **Scholarly/provenance research** — optional but adds value; academic contacts above

**For formal written appraisals:** Seek an appraiser certified by:
- American Society of Appraisers (ASA) — asappraisers.org
- Appraisers Association of America (AAA) — appraisersassociation.org
Search both directories for "Asian decorative arts" specialty.

## Key Points to Communicate to Any Appraiser

1. Four of five pieces have intact spirit cavities (藏腹) — significant authenticity marker
2. Statues 1, 2, 4, and 5 likely form a coherent altar ensemble — value as group exceeds sum of parts
3. Statue 2 reverse has possible painted inscription/marks — warrants expert examination
4. There is also a carved wooden architectural panel (flame/cloud motifs) visible in photos — possibly same temple origin
5. Do NOT clean, consolidate, or restore before appraisal
