• Two kneeling armored warrior attendants with spirit cavities (藏腹) on the reverse

I am based in Seattle and would welcome the opportunity to discuss next steps — whether that's an in-person visit, a home evaluation, or an initial review of photographs. I am interested in both a formal appraisal and understanding whether the pieces might be suitable for auction.
