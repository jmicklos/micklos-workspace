## Key decisions & rationale
- **Identification over valuation.** Jonathan wants to understand what these are before deciding anything. The keep/sell calculus depends on cultural context, not dollar value.
- **Don't assert AI-generated identities to experts.** Emails describe what we observe and ask for help — no "Ming dynasty" or specific deity names. Let experts form their own conclusions without anchoring.
- **Material noted honestly.** Pieces 01-03 feel like wood, 04-05 feel like plaster/clay. Past Sonnet analysis assumed all were wood — we corrected this.
- **Dropped jargon.** "Polychrome" replaced with "carved and painted" in all outward-facing text.
- **Numbering cross-reference matters.** My 01=their 03, my 02=their 05, my 03=their 04, my 04=their 02, my 05=their 01. This caused significant photo misorganization that was fixed.
