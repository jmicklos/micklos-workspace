## 2026-06-07 / 2026-06-08 — Initial cataloging, research, and expert outreach

### Situation
Jonathan has five Chinese carved figures bought from a gallery in Thailand in Feb 2023. He'd never documented them. A prior Claude Sonnet session had analyzed 34 photos and produced detailed per-piece writeups (delivered as a zip file), but Jonathan wasn't sure if that analysis was correct. He wanted everything organized, researched, and then to reach out to experts for proper identification. The identification drives keep/sell decisions — his fiancee Wan Ting is uneasy about having potentially religious temple objects as decor.

### Approach & Rationale
1. Jonathan sent new photos of each piece with measurements. I created an initial project and catalog.
2. He provided the zip of past Sonnet analysis. I unzipped it, cross-referenced the two numbering systems by visually comparing photos, and consolidated everything.
3. I initially got the cross-reference between pieces 01 and 03 wrong (swapped Zhong Kui and the seated civil deity). Jonathan caught the photo mismatch and we fixed it. Root cause: the new photos were rotated 90 degrees and I misidentified the pieces from single angles.
4. Restructured from one big note into per-piece directories (each with .md + photos/).
5. Researched auction comparables — found the market is soft/niche, $500-$3,000 per piece range.
6. Drafted and sent outreach emails to two local experts (Prof. Wang at UW, Dr. Foong Ping at SAM). Kept emails honest — described observations without asserting AI-generated identities or dates.
7. Dr. Foong Ping responded same day with a polite decline. Prof. Wang still pending.

### What Jonathan Said
- "I'd prefer identification as the priority."
- On Wan Ting: "she's a bit uneasy about them... kind of WTF why do you have these... she's less happy about them if they are religious. Perhaps if we can get some context on them such that we don't feel like they give off 'look at this random place in asia I mini colonized' vibes."
- On material: "some are definitely wood, some feel like some sort of plaster" — pieces 01-03 wood, 04-05 plaster/clay.
- "I think going forward I want to differentiate my projects that are for the home vs projects that are for my house" — hence "Stuff" prefix.
- On past context: "I don't know for certain if that context is correct or not, I need you to help figure it out. But the photos are authentic."
- Called out photo misorganization twice — "dude, these are NOT organized correctly."
- On jargon: "what do we mean by polychrome? The text is a bit confusing."
