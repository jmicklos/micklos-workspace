## Zhong Kui Specific

No confirmed auction price found for a polychrome **wood** Zhong Kui with demon base. Closest:
- Earthenware Zhong Kui on tiger (19th/20th C.) — €179 at Coronari Auctions
- Bamboo carving of Zhong Kui, 17th/18th C. — estimated £5,000-£8,000 at Bonhams London, May 2025 (realized price not public)
- Wood Zhong Kui figures on eBay/Etsy: $50-$500 for later/reproduction pieces

The absence of comparables could mean this type rarely comes to market, which is potentially interesting for a specialist collector.

## Key Takeaways

- **Artemis Fine Arts** is the most active auction house for this category. Their realized prices for Ming-Qing wood deity/official figures cluster at $600-$3,000.
- **Warrior/guardian figures** have a thin recent record — most either passed or sold sub-$500 at regional houses.
- **Pair premiums** appear negligible in the data.
- **Bonhams passing makes sense** — these are $500-$3,000 pieces at auction, below their typical threshold.
- **This is a soft, niche market.** The right specialist buyer matters more than the auction venue.
