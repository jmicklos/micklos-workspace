*Last updated: 2026-06-09*

## What this project is
Mount a Samsung Frame TV in the alcove above the living room fireplace and set up audio — part of the Tudor Revival studs-out remodel in Capitol Hill, Seattle. Child project of [[Home – Remodel – Finish]]. Due June 30, 2026.

- **Audio decision: open.** Two viable paths remain, no final call yet.
- **No hardware purchased yet.** Need physical measurements before buying.
- **Project files:** `Home – Remodel – Living Room TV.md` (project note), `worklog.md` (session log), this file. Tasks float up to the Living Room section of `Home – Remodel – Finish/Home – Remodel – Finish.md`.

## Key decisions & rationale
- **Frame TV chosen** because Jonathan already has one in the basement and knows the One Invisible Connection cable is thin enough to route along the white fireplace surround.
- **One Connect box at floor level** to one side of the fireplace — all HDMI sources plug in there, keeping cables away from the alcove.
- **No Smurf tube** exists from the alcove to either side — this is the core constraint. Alcove has power + Ethernet only. Any cable between the alcove and floor level must run externally along the surround.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Sonos Port/optical (~75ms latency), AirPlay (latency + single-input), in-ceiling speakers (too much construction), media console below (fireplace goes to floor), off-center soundbar placement (Jonathan explicitly against), soundbar mounted to TV (Samsung doesn't support it), Leon FrameBar (~$4k, still needs wired connection).
- **Q-Symphony is Samsung-only** — no third-party soundbar can wirelessly connect to a Samsung TV. This was confirmed via research.

## Jonathan's preferences
- "Sonos is a known entity with known quality audio and I don't want to spend a bunch of money buying a new system"
- Completely against off-center soundbar placement or hiding it in furniture to the side
- Completely against visible draped HDMI cables — but accepted a slim white HDMI along the white surround is pragmatic if going Sonos
- Wants clean, minimal cabling — fewer cables is better
- Fireplace area has no furniture yet — furniture will be purchased around the AV setup, not the other way around

## Open threads
- **Audio decision is the blocker.** Two paths:
  - **Sonos Beam ($450):** HDMI eARC to One Connect box. Stays in Sonos ecosystem. 2 cables along surround (One Invisible Connection + slim white HDMI eARC).
  - **Samsung S801D ($800) or QS90H ($1,000):** Wireless Q-Symphony to TV. 1 cable along surround. Leaves Sonos ecosystem. S801D is ultra-slim (1.4" tall, fits narrow shelf). QS90H is all-in-one with no external sub but standard-size bar (need to confirm mantel depth).
- Samsung Q-series soundbars reviewed well overall; QS90H got AVS Forum Top Choice 2026.
- No other brands offer wireless TV-to-soundbar connection with Samsung TVs.
