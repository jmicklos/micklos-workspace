# Home – Remodel – Living Room TV

## Goal
Mount a TV above the living room fireplace with clean audio integration.

## Plan

### Audio decision pending
Two options under consideration:

**Option A: Sonos Beam (stays in Sonos ecosystem)**
- Sonos Beam on the mantel shelf, connected via HDMI eARC to One Connect box
- Two cables along surround: One Invisible Connection + slim white HDMI eARC
- Pro: integrates with existing Sonos system
- Con: extra visible cable along surround

**Option B: Samsung wireless soundbar (zero cables to TV)**
- Samsung S801D ($800, ultra-slim 1.4" tall) or QS90H ($1,000, all-in-one no sub) on the mantel
- Connects wirelessly to Frame TV via Q-Symphony — only One Invisible Connection cable along surround
- Pro: one fewer cable, purpose-built for Frame TV
- Con: separate audio ecosystem from Sonos

### Setup (decided)
- **TV:** Samsung Frame TV mounted in the alcove above the fireplace
- **Source hub:** Samsung One Connect box hidden to one side of the fireplace (all HDMI sources plug in here)
- **Cable routing:** One Invisible Connection along the white surround edge (+ HDMI if Sonos)

### Constraints
- Alcove has power + Ethernet but no Smurf tube to either side — cannot run cables through the wall
- Fireplace goes to floor level — no space for a media console below
- Firebox is gas insert
