# Borneo Honeymoon — Itinerary v1 (built to win over Wan Ting)

*Draft 2026-08-01. Sabah-core route (smoother logistics + more comfort than adding Sarawak/Mulu). ~13 days / 12 nights. Singapore gateway.*

## The strategy (why it's sequenced this way)

Wan Ting has *actually* dealt with leeches; Jonathan hasn't. So this itinerary is NOT sold by pretending the jungle is a spa. It's sold by three moves:

1. **Soft landing.** Start on a luxury island, not in the mud. She arrives relaxed and trusting before any grit.
2. **Bounded grit, cushioned on both sides.** Only ~3 days of true jungle (Danum). Luxury before, river-comfort after. The hard part has a clear start and end.
3. **End on the highest note.** The trip *closes* on romance (island/water bungalow), so the lasting memory is candlelight, not tiger leeches. Recency wins.

Bonus frame for the pitch: **Wan Ting is the expert here.** She's the one who's survived the jungle — this is her teaching her soft Seattle husband how to handle her half of the world. That's a role, not a hardship.

**Timing:** Target **May–June 2027** — Sabah's drier season. Drier = *materially fewer leeches* and better trekking/diving. This is an honest selling point, not spin.

## The route

### Leg A — Soft landing: Gaya Island, 2 nights
- **Day 1:** Singapore → Kota Kinabalu (~2h40 flight). Speedboat to **Gaya Island Resort** (YTL luxury, in Tunku Abdul Rahman Marine Park). Arrive to sunset, welcome drink, spa. Zero grit. She lands soft.
- **Day 2:** Reef snorkeling, beach, **couples spa**, private beach dinner. This is the "we're actually on a honeymoon" anchor before the adventure.

### Leg B — The wild heart: Danum Valley, 3 nights
- **Day 3:** Fly KK → Lahad Datu (~50 min), ~2.5h drive to **Borneo Rainforest Lodge** — deep in 130-million-year-old *primary* rainforest, but genuinely a lodge: private veranda, hot water, good food, a naturalist per outing. Evening night drive (civets, slow loris, maybe clouded leopard tracks).
- **Day 4:** Pre-dawn **canopy walkway** as the gibbons start calling and mist sits in the valley; **wild orangutan** trekking; jungle trails; night walk. Private jacuzzi on the deck after.
- **Day 5:** Waterfall swim, birding (Bornean specialties), or just laze on the deck over the canopy. **Private jungle dinner** set up by the river.
- ⚠️ **This is the leech leg.** Honest version below — but it's 3 days, and it's the ONLY leg with leeches.

### Leg C — The river (comfort returns): Kinabatangan, 2 nights
- **Day 6:** Road transfer to the **Kinabatangan River** (Sukau). Everything here is **boat-based → essentially leech-free** (relief valve right after Danum). Afternoon cruise: proboscis monkeys, hornbills, macaques on the banks. After dark, the **firefly cruise** — mangroves pulsing like a slow strobe. Deeply romantic, zero effort.
- **Day 7:** Dawn and dusk river cruises; oxbow lake; pygmy elephants if the herd is around. Slow, wildlife-dense, comfortable.

### Leg D — Warm water + romance closer, 4 nights
Pick the ending based on whether you want **pure romance** or **diving**:

**Option 1 — Pure romance (recommended for the Wan Ting sell):**
- **Day 8–11:** Fly to **Lankayan Island** (remote eco-luxury, Sugud Islands) *or* back to **Gaya / Bunga Raya**. Overwater/beachfront, turtles nesting, sunset dinners, nothing on the schedule but each other. The trip peaks on romance.

**Option 2 — Diver's finish:**
- **Day 8–11:** Fly KK → Tawau, transfer to **Semporna**, boat to **Kapalai** (water bungalows) or **Mabul** — base for **Sipadan**, one of the best dive sites on Earth (turtle-and-barracuda walls). Sipadan permits are limited (~176/day) and must be booked well ahead.

- **Day 12/13:** Fly KK → Singapore → home. (Natural chance to bookend with Wan Ting's family in SG.)

### Optional add-on module — Gunung Mulu (Sarawak), +2–3 nights
If you want the signature **Deer Cave bat exodus** (millions of bats spiraling out at dusk), the **Pinnacles**, and the world's largest cave chamber — it's spectacular but adds a separate Sarawak flight cluster. Slot it *before* Danum or make it a v2 decision. Left out of the core route to keep logistics kind to Wan Ting.

## The leech question — the honest version (because she'll know if you fudge it)

- **Where:** Only Danum Valley (and Mulu if added). Islands, river, hot springs, KK = leech-free.
- **When:** They spike in the wet. **May–June (drier) = far fewer.** Timing is the single biggest lever.
- **Harm:** None. Bornean leeches carry **no disease** (unlike mosquitoes). They're a gross-out, not a health risk. Worst case: an itchy spot.
- **Defense:** Leech socks (lodge provides), pants tucked, repellent, check at breaks. Wan Ting already knows the drill — Jonathan is the trainee.
- **Reframe:** 3 bounded days, harmless, dry-season-minimized, sandwiched between a spa island and a firefly river. That's the deal.

## Romance set-pieces already baked in
Couples spa (Gaya) · private beach dinner · pre-dawn canopy walkway together · private jungle dinner by the river (Danum) · firefly night cruise (Kinabatangan) · overwater/beach sunset finish (Lankayan/Kapalai). Escalating, and the biggest ones land at the **end**.

## Logistics backbone
- Gateway: **Singapore → Kota Kinabalu** (~2h40). KK is the Sabah hub for all internal flights.
- Internal: KK↔Lahad Datu (Danum), KK↔Sandakan (Kinabatangan/Lankayan), KK↔Tawau (Sipadan). All short hops.
- ~12 nights core; +2–3 if Mulu added.
