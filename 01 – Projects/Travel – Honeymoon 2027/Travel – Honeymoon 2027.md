## Options Slate (March 2027) — discovery reopened 2026-08-01

Framework had pre-narrowed to Bhutan vs Patagonia; reopened to survey the full field against "extreme meets bougie" + March seasonality.

**Best March fit (clean framework match):**
1. **Patagonia (Chilean)** — Torres del Paine treks + Explora Salto Chico / Tierra / Awasi. Late southern summer, prime trails, dramatic light.
2. **New Zealand (Fiordland / Southern Alps)** — heli-hiking, Milford/Routeburn, glaciers + Minaret Station (heli-only lodge) / Blanket Bay / Matakauri. Late summer, **no altitude**, safest weather.
3. **Namibia (Sossusvlei)** — the literal conceptual model. Dunes + balloon + andBeyond Desert Lodge / Little Kulala. Green-season tail = dramatic skies, fewer people.
4. **Bhutan** — Tiger's Nest + Jomolhari + Amankora / Six Senses. Rhododendrons, Paro Tshechu.

**Strong with one caveat:**
5. **Nepal (Annapurna/Everest)** — spring trek season + Dwarika's / luxury lodge-treks / heli. Caveat: serious altitude.
6. **Atacama (Chile)** — volcanoes/salt flats + Explora / Awasi / Tierra. Caveat: altitude (4,000m+ excursions); more landscape than trek.
7. **Carretera Austral / Chilean fjords** — self-drive expedition, Marble Caves; pairs with Patagonia lodges. Caveat: road-trip, less single-lodge anchored.

**Wildcards:**
- **Antarctica** — ultra-bougie expedition ships, whales; but March is season tail, window tight.
- **Morocco (High Atlas + Sahara)** — Kasbah Tamadot + luxury desert camp; but Toubkal is a *winter* climb in March.

**Walked back (fail March seasonality):** Faroe & Lofoten (winter, thin lodging), Mongolia (deep winter), Georgia (Caucasus trekking is summer-only).

**Honesty flags:**

## Timing + Romance reframe (2026-08-01)

Expanded the window to **March–June 2027** and evolved the framework to add romance.

**Framework evolution:** "extreme meets bougie" → **"Adventure → Recovery → Romance."** The missing honeymoon ingredient is a warm, slow, just-the-two-of-us leg. Deliver as a **two-leg bookend trip**: a hard/dramatic leg + a warm romance leg. This also hedges the pregnancy question — dial the adventure leg to whatever's appropriate, keep the romance leg always safe. March didn't allow this (warm legs out of season); May/June does.

**How May/June reshuffles seasonality:**
- *Better:* Namibia/Sossusvlei (peak dry desert), Atacama, Bhutan (May only — trek opens).
- *Worse:* Patagonia (top lodges close ~end April; May = fall-color shoulder only, verify closures; June = winter), New Zealand (autumn→winter), Bhutan June / Nepal June (monsoon).
- *Newly unlocked:* Iceland / Lofoten / Norway (midnight sun — the March walk-backs come roaring back), East Africa (Kilimanjaro, Rwanda gorillas, safari — June dry season), Peru/Machu Picchu (May/June dry).

**Bookend concepts (adventure leg + romance leg):**
- **Namibia + Indian Ocean** ⭐ — Sossusvlei dunes + balloon (andBeyond Desert Lodge) → Mnemba Island (Zanzibar) or Benguerra (Mozambique). Both legs peak May/June; **no altitude**. Standout: the conceptual model + full framework satisfied with zero caveats.
- **East Africa classic** — Kilimanjaro climb *or* Rwanda gorilla trek (Singita Kwitonda / Bisate) → Zanzibar or Serengeti Singita. June dry.
- **Iceland (single-country bookend)** — Highlands self-drive / heli-hike / glacier (Deplar Farm, Highland Base) → The Retreat at Blue Lagoon thermal-spa. June midnight sun, inherently romantic.
- **Peru** — Salkantay/Inca Trail to Machu Picchu at dawn → Sacred Valley luxury (Explora Valle Sagrado) + Andean Explorer sleeper train. May/June dry. ⚠ altitude.
- **Bhutan + Maldives** — Tiger's Nest + Jomolhari (opens May) → Maldives overwater. May only (June = monsoon both).

**Romance set-pieces (portable to any option):** sunrise balloon + champagne breakfast · star bed under the Milky Way (andBeyond Namibia) · private heli-picnic on a summit/glacier · private guide + villa for a genuinely-alone leg (Awasi model) · dedicated couples-spa/thermal-soak day as couple-time · overwater/private-island closer.

**Tension to weigh:** a two-leg bookend runs longer (~12–16 days) and costs more than a single destination — shapes budget + time-off. May/June East Africa + Iceland book 9–12 months out, so the 2026-11-01 deadline is right on time for June.

## Expanded slate + romance thesis (2026-08-01) — "we will go ANYWHERE"

Jonathan removed the constraints ("we will go ANYWHERE") and asked to expand the field and sell it, provoked by: "can backpacking through the jungles of Borneo be romantic?"

**Romance thesis (the reframe):** For an adventurous couple, romance isn't candlelight — it's *shared awe* and *shared hardship that becomes a 50-year story*. The sweat isn't the enemy of romance; it's what makes the plunge pool after mean something. This unlocks jungle/expedition destinations as legitimately romantic. Answer to the Borneo question: **yes.**

**Expanded hero options (sold on concrete set-pieces):**
- **Borneo** ⭐ (featured) — Danum Valley primary rainforest + **Borneo Rainforest Lodge** (bougie-in-the-jungle proof: canopy-veranda, jacuzzi, dawn canopy walkway, wild orangutans) → Mulu (Deer Cave bat exodus, Pinnacles, Sarawak Chamber) → Kinabatangan River (fireflies, pygmy elephants) → optional Mt Kinabalu via ferrata / Sipadan diving. **Key hook: it's Wan Ting's home region (short hop from Singapore), folds her world into the marriage. Extreme + bougie + romance, all satisfied.**
- **Namibia + Zanzibar** ⭐ — still the standout bookend (dunes/balloon/star bed → Mnemba private island). Both legs peak May/June, no altitude.
- **Raja Ampat (Misool)** — most biodiverse seas on Earth, private-island eco-luxury, romance by isolation.
- **The Kimberley (Berkeley River Lodge)** — seaplane-only, Horizontal Falls, ancient gorges. May/June dry season = perfect.
- **Svalbard** — June midnight sun, luxury expedition ship, polar bears + warm cabin.
- **Madagascar** — Tsingy (limestone-blade via ferrata) + Avenue of Baobabs at sunset (unambiguously romantic).
- **Ethiopia (Danakil/Simien/Lalibela)** — the "anywhere" trump card: Erta Ale lava lake, Dallol, geladas, rock churches. Least bougie, most singular.
- **Kumano Kodo (Japan)** — gentle counterpoint: 1,000-yr pilgrimage trail hut-to-hut + private-onsen ryokans. Romance-forward, adventure seasoned in.
- **Galápagos** — ~~Wan Ting's addition, 2026-08-02~~ → **DEFERRED to a future family trip (2026-08-02).** Jonathan's pushback: Galápagos is a *great travel-with-kids* opportunity (fearless close-up wildlife, safe, no altitude/malaria, family departures, snorkeling magic at any age) — and it loses almost nothing by waiting. Spending a scarce **kid-incompatible** honeymoon slot on the one option that's a *gift to the future family* is backwards. Frame to Wan Ting as "not no — *later*, and as something better." Reallocated to the family-travel bucket. *(Detail retained: shifts framework to "wonder meets bougie"; Mar–May prime; Aqua Mare/Flora/Silversea/Pikaia; pairs with Mashpi Lodge; Zika flag for Ecuador if pregnant.)*
