# Honeymoon 2027 — Options to Review Together 💍

*For Jonathan + Wan Ting. Draft 2026-08-09. Goal: react, veto, and pick a direction — not lock a place yet.*

## The shape we've agreed on
- **When:** sometime **March–June 2027** (after the Dec wedding + Feb Singapore tea ceremony).
- **What it is:** an **adventure + luxury + romance** trip — a real experience by day, a beautiful place to recover and be together by night.
- **The one rule:** it's a **without-kids trip.** Litmus test → *"Would we happily do this with a 6-year-old?"* If **yes**, we save it for the family years. If **no**, it's exactly what this honeymoon is for.
- **Already decided:** **Galápagos → saved for a future family trip** (it's *better* with kids). **Iceland → out** (we've already done it together). We're not chasing another Serengeti migration — we peaked on that in Tanzania.

## The real fork: what kind of romance do we want?
1. **Wild-awe** — the thrill of being the only two people out there.
2. **Grand-landscape** — epic scenery + serious luxury staging.
3. **Slow-intimate** — romance-first, with adventure woven in gently.

## The candidates

### Wild-awe
| Place | The dream image | The romance | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Borneo** 🌿 | Dawn on a jungle canopy walkway as gibbons call; millions of bats pour out of a cave at dusk | Fireflies on the river, a plunge pool over the rainforest — *and it honors Wan Ting's home region* | Leeches (bounded to ~3 days, dry-season-minimized, harmless) |
| **Rwanda gorillas** 🦍 | An hour sitting a few feet from a silverback and his family | Ultra-luxury lodges (Singita, Bisate); a wholly *new* wildlife high | Permits are 15+, book far ahead; pricey |
| **French Polynesia (wild version)** 🐆 | Drifting a wall of sharks at Fakarava; hiking the remote Marquesas | Ends on warm-water island romance — the real thing, not a resort cliché | Long-haul; diving-led |

### Grand-landscape
| Place | The dream image | The romance | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Namibia + Zanzibar** ⭐ | Sunrise balloon over red dunes → barefoot private island | Champagne on the dunes, a bed under the whole Milky Way, then a week of just us | Two legs = longer + pricier |
| **Patagonia** 🏔️ | The granite towers of Torres del Paine at first light | Luxury lodge, fireside, dramatic light | Our window (May/June) is late — top lodges start closing |
| **Bolivia — Uyuni salt flats** 🪞 | Sleeping in a glass dome on a mirror of sky | Surreal, wildly photogenic, unlike anywhere | High altitude |

### Slow-intimate
| Place | The dream image | The romance | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kumano Kodo (Japan)** ⛩️ | Walking a 1,000-year-old pilgrimage trail through cedar forest | A private hot-spring ryokan and kaiseki dinner every night | Gentle — less "extreme" |

2. Any instant **veto**? Any instant **yes**?
3. Pick your **top 2** places. Jonathan does the same. We compare.
