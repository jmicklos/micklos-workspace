# ADU Furnishing Inventory Audit
*Unit: 2 bedroom / 1 bathroom / kitchen + living/dining. Basement ADU.*
*Last updated: 2026-07-02*

## Purpose
Full audit of what a rentable unit needs, split by the two rental models under consideration. Use this to build the shopping list once the model is chosen (or to provision for both — Airbnb is a superset of furnished-apartment needs).

## Legend
- **F** = expected for a **Furnished Apartment** (corporate housing / traveling nurse, mid-to-long term)
- **A** = expected for an **Airbnb** (short-term, hospitality standard)
- **Status:** `have` = already owned · `planned` = decided/researched, not purchased · `need` = gap to fill · `?` = needs confirmation

> **Core distinction:** Furnished-apartment tenants live there and typically supply their own consumables/toiletries after move-in — you provide durable goods + a fully equipped kitchen + 1–2 linen sets. Airbnb layers hospitality on top: consumables restocked every turnover, 2+ linen/towel sets, toiletries, welcome items, decor, self check-in, and a house manual.

## 7. Furnished-Apartment Emphasis
*What matters more for mid/long-term tenants (nurses, corporate).*

- [ ] **Workspace** — desk + comfortable chair + good lighting + reliable outlets. Highest-leverage add for this market.
- [ ] Ample storage — dresser + closet + hangers so a multi-week stay can unpack
- [ ] Fully equipped kitchen (they cook real meals, not just reheat)
- [ ] In-unit or easy laundry access
- [ ] Durable, replaceable goods over precious decor
- [ ] Reliable high-speed internet + clear parking info
- [ ] 1–2 linen sets (tenant handles their own laundry, less turnover buffer needed)

## Smoke / CO detector requirements — resolved

### Smoke — current state (confirmed 2026-07-02)
- Installed (all **hardwired**): main bedroom (**combo smoke/CO**), BR2 (smoke), common area (smoke). **Still to install: stairwell smoke detector.**
- **Bedroom combo STAYS put:** the gas **water heater** is in a utility closet accessed through the main bedroom → fuel-burning appliance "within the bedroom," so CO is required in-room. Cannot swap it out to the common area.
- **Hardwired vs. battery:** existing homes may use battery (10-yr sealed); **new construction / substantial remodel requires hardwired + battery backup + interconnected.** ⚠️ The studs-out remodel likely triggers the hardwired+interconnected standard for the ADU — **verify with GC/electrician/permit scope**.

### CO — compulsory vs. smart
**Compulsory (legally required):**
- CO alarm required — TWO independent triggers: (1) it's a rental (RCW 19.27.530, rentals not exempt); (2) the unit has a **gas fireplace** (fuel-burning appliance independently triggers CO requirement).
- **Placement minimum:** outside each sleeping area (immediate vicinity of bedrooms) + at least one per level. Single-level ADU → **one CO alarm in the common area outside the bedrooms satisfies the legal minimum.**
- Must be **UL 2034** listed. Owner installs, working at move-in.
- **CO is NOT legally required to be hardwired or interconnected** — battery/plug-in CO is legal (unlike smoke).

**Smart (recommended, not required):**
- Combo smoke/CO units — CO rides along for free since smoke is already being hardwired/interconnected.
- Add a CO alarm in the living area near the **gas fireplace** (5–15 ft away per manufacturer, to avoid nuisance).
- Hardwire/interconnect the CO so a 3am trip wakes a sleeping guest.
- Optional: CO inside bedrooms; 10-yr sealed battery if going battery.

### Product match (existing alarms = Kidde FireX SMACFEX)
- Existing smoke alarms are **Kidde FireX SMACFEX** (21033081) — hardwired, AA backup, twist-and-click bracket, FireX harness.
- **Compatible combo smoke/CO = Kidde FireX CUAC\*FEX line** (CUACFEX, CUACFEX-V, CUAC10YFEX, CUAC10YFEX-V). Same FireX bracket + harness + interconnect — direct swap, no adapter. Pick: **CUAC10YFEX** (10-yr sealed battery) or **-V** for voice ("Fire!"/"Carbon Monoxide!"), best for a rental.
- ⚠️ Do NOT cross to Kidde "classic" line (KN-COSM-IBA etc.) — different harness.
- **Install plan (final, all confirmed):** all ADU alarms are **hardwired**. Main-bedroom combo stays (water heater in-room). Buy **1 CUAC10YFEX combo for the common area** (gas fireplace + outside sleeping areas); **relocate the displaced common-area smoke unit to the stairwell** (no stairwell purchase needed). Net detector spend ~$60 (one combo). BR2 keeps smoke-only. Final layout: bedroom = combo, BR2 = smoke, common area = combo, stairwell = relocated smoke.
- **Separate code flag:** gas water heater accessed from a sleeping room must be direct-vent/sealed-combustion or in a solid self-closing-door closet with dedicated combustion air — verify GC handled this (combustion-air/appliance-location issue, not a CO-alarm issue).

- **Not a "stairwell" rule** — it's per-level and per-unit. The basement ADU is its own unit/level and needs full self-contained coverage; the ground-floor top-of-stairwell is part of the main-house level.
- Reference: [RCW 19.27.530](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=19.27.530); Seattle SDCI Tip #317.
