# ADU Shopping List

*Last updated: 2026-07-02*

Derived from [[Furnishing Inventory Audit]]. Prices are **conservative (padded)** per Jonathan's "double the estimate to be safe" rule — treat as upper-bound planning numbers, not quotes. Only items not already owned are listed.

## How to read the "Needed for" column
- **Both** — buy regardless of rental model.
- **Airbnb** — hospitality layer; skip if you go furnished/mid-term only.
- **Furnished** — matters primarily for mid-term tenants (nurses/corporate); optional for Airbnb.

> **Recommendation:** spec to the **Airbnb standard** (buy the "Both" + "Airbnb" items). Airbnb's needs are a superset of the furnished-rental needs, so this keeps *both* models open. The desk (Furnished) is still worth adding — it's the highest-leverage item for the mid-term market and cheap insurance.
