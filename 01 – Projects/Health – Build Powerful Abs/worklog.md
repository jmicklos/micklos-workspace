# Worklog — Health – Build Powerful Abs

## 2026-08-10 — Project kickoff & diagnosis

### Situation
- Jonathan wants **permanently visible, thick abs** — a "powerful core strength" look, explicitly NOT fight-club-skinny shredded.
- Long history: he lifts heavy (1000lb club — DL ~500, squat 285×6, bench ~250 1RM recovering from injury) but has **NEVER built a visible six-pack**, even dieting down to ~6% BF. No significant abs at that leanness.
- Trigger: after a long day of rowing in Alaska, his upper abs "popped" in a powerful-core way. He wants that permanent. He already suspected "1000 situps a day" is the wrong answer — correct.

### Approach & Rationale
- **Diagnosis:** fat is NOT his limiter (he's proven he can get to 6% with no abs) — **rectus abdominis thickness is**. He's been *revealing* a muscle he never *built*. Flip the order: **build first (at ~12–15% BF), reveal later.**
- **Train abs as a muscle for hypertrophy:** progressive overload, loaded movements, 8–15 reps near failure — NOT high-rep endurance. Situps also fail because (a) endurance stimulus, (b) hip-flexor hijack vs. true spinal flexion, (c) no progressive overload.
- His heavy compounds give elite bracing but ~zero direct rectus hypertrophy — that's the gap this project fills.
- Rejected: hard dieting now (would blunt muscle growth); high-rep bodyweight work (wrong stimulus).

### Decisions locked with Jonathan (via clarifying Qs)
- **Frequency:** 2 days/week (tack onto existing lifting days).
- **Equipment:** full gym (cable stack, hanging bar, machines available).
- **Timeline:** 12-week build block → reassess ~2026-11-02. Framed as a PARA project with a done state (finish block + reassess), not an open-ended habit.
