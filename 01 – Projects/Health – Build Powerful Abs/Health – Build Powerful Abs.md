# Health – Build Powerful Abs

## Goal
Build **permanently visible, thick abs** — a "powerful core" look, not shredded fight-club leanness. Done state: complete a 12-week loaded ab-hypertrophy block (through **2026-11-02**), then reassess thickness/visibility and decide whether to run a second block or transition to a reveal (cut).

## The core insight (why past attempts failed)
Jonathan has repeatedly dieted to ~6% BF with **no significant abs**. That proves fat is NOT the limiter — **rectus abdominis thickness is**. Years were spent *revealing* a muscle that was never *built*. This block flips the order: **build the muscle first, reveal it later** at a sustainable ~12–15% BF.

Corollary: **abs are skeletal muscle** — they grow via progressive overload (load, 8–15 reps, near failure), NOT via high-rep endurance work (1000 situps builds endurance, not cross-sectional area). His 500 deadlift gives elite *bracing* strength but almost zero direct rectus hypertrophy stimulus. That gap is the whole project.

What's genetic vs. trainable:
- **Genetic (can't change):** number of ab "blocks," their symmetry/alignment (tendinous intersections).
- **Trainable (the target):** thickness and definition of the bellies.

## The Program — 2×/week, full gym, 12 weeks

**Method:** double progression. Stay in the listed rep range, 1–3 reps in reserve. When you hit the TOP of the range on all sets, add load next session; then work reps back up from the bottom.

### Session A (e.g. Mon)
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weighted cable crunch (kneeling, rope) | 3 × 10–15 | #1 mass builder. Crunch ribs to pelvis, spinal flexion — not a hip hinge. Load it like a real lift. |
| Hanging leg raise (progress knee → straight → DB between feet) | 3 × 8–12 | Lower rectus. Control the lower, no swinging. Add a dumbbell once bodyweight is easy. |
| Ab wheel rollout (standing angle scales difficulty) | 2 × 8–12 | Loaded anti-extension + flexion. Bigger lean = harder. |

### Session B (e.g. Thu)
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weighted decline / machine crunch | 3 × 10–15 | Loaded spinal flexion, full stretch at bottom. |
| Hanging leg raise (weighted as able) | 3 × 8–12 | Repeat — lower rectus responds to frequency. |
| Weighted Pallof press or cable woodchop | 2 × 10–12/side | Light oblique/anti-rotation work for a complete, "powerful" midsection. |

**Rules that matter:**
- **Full ROM:** posterior pelvic tilt, crunch ribs down, full stretch → full contraction. Load the stretched position.
- **Progress every week:** add reps or load. Log it (see worklog).
- **~10–14 hard sets/week total** — plenty at 2×/week for a novel stimulus.
- Keep smashing this even during any future cut.

## Nutrition posture
- **Do NOT diet hard right now.** Build at maintenance or a slight surplus (~12–15% BF is fine) so the muscle actually grows. Adequate protein (~1g/lb).
- Reveal (cut) comes AFTER thickness is built — likely a later, separate project.
