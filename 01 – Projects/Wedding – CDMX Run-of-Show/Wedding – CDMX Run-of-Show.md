# Wedding – CDMX Run-of-Show

> **Date:** December 12, 2026
> **Venue:** Casino Español — Salón Los Reyes, Isabel la Católica, Centro Histórico, CDMX
> **Guest count:** ~80
> **Wedding party:** 6 groomsmen + 5 bridesmaids (11 total)

## Definition of Done
Locked minute-by-minute schedule distributed to coordinator, EFEGE (photo/video), DJ, and venue. All stakeholders aligned, no open blocking decisions.

## Hard Constraints
- **Sunset:** ~6:00 PM CDMX (standard time, no DST). Golden hour ~5:00–6:00 PM; usable twilight to ~6:20.
- **Venue layout:** Main floor = restaurant + ceremony space (shared egress, not shared room). One floor up = cocktail hour + reception (Salón Los Reyes). Restaurant operates until 6:00 PM; fully exclusive from 6:00 PM onward.
- **Guest flow:** Guests arrive main floor → ceremony → go upstairs for cocktail hour → stay upstairs for reception + dancing. Couple stays on main floor briefly after ceremony for golden-hour portraits, then joins upstairs.
- **Photo coverage:** EFEGE, 10 hours. If starts 1:00 PM → ends 11:00 PM (final DJ hour uncovered).
- **DJ:** 6-hour block, planned 6:00 PM–midnight. Cannot cover both a pre-6PM ceremony AND dancing to midnight without extension.

## Decided
- **First look:** YES — protects golden-hour light regardless of ceremony placement
- **First dance:** early, in wedding gown, at/right after grand entrance
- **Grand entrance:** introduce wedding party in PAIRS (not individually), then the couple, straight into first dance
- **Dress change:** bride changes into a secondary dress (NOT qipao — qipao is for Singapore). Timing TBD; traditional options are between main course and dessert, or after dinner before dancing. ~15–20 min needed.
- **Yum seng:** included, led by Jin Rui (agreed ✓), during dinner after main course. ~30 sec moment, not a speech.
- **Toasts:** spaced between courses, one per course break. 3 speeches (possibly 4 if Dan wants to). Running order TBD — finalize closer to wedding after conferring with speakers.
  - Athul Acharya — best man toast (~3–4 min)
  - Gabe Brown — best man toast (~3–4 min)
  - Maid of honor — bride's side toast (~3–4 min)
  - Dan Micklos — father of bride (confirmed ✓) (~2–3 min)
  - Jin Rui Lee — yum seng (~30 sec)
  - Jonathan + Wan Ting — thank-you toast (~30 sec–1 min)
  - Draft spread: one speech after each of the first 3 courses, yum seng + dad (if yes) + couple thank-you after main / before dessert
- **Dinner:** ~30 min per course, ~2 hrs total (toasts fill the gaps). Casino Español in-house four-course plated: starter, mid-course, main, dessert.
- **Cookie table:** three origins — commercially packaged Pittsburgh cookies (Bethel/Oakmont Bakery or similar) + CDMX local bakery (pan dulce, polvorones, cocadas) + commercially packaged Singapore cookies/treats. All must be factory-sealed for Mexico customs (no homemade imports allowed).
- **Cake:** alongside cookie table; cake-cutting folded in, not staged
- **No bouquet/garter toss**
- **Close:** soft, intentional — deliberate final song announced by DJ, no staged exit
- **Tea ceremony / gate-crashing:** deferred to Singapore celebration (April 2027)
- **Officiant:** Lauren (bride's sister), custom non-religious ceremony, mic'd. She's writing it herself — let her drive the creative direction. Default: no readings, no unity rituals, clean format (processional → words → vows → rings → kiss → recessional). But if she has a great idea, open to it. Target: ~20–25 min.
- **Cocktail hour:** 60 min, one drink + canapés
- **Guest comms:** Email for day-of logistics and CDMX guidance (may revisit for WhatsApp broadcast later)
