# Home – Add a Shed

Add a prefab, DIY-assembled shed to the property at [[1120 19th Ave E]] for outdoor and household storage. Related to [[Home – Grounds]] (siting must not conflict with the grounds plan / future fence line).

## Definition of Done
A prefab shed kit is sited, assembled, and loaded — with **outdoor implements** on open shelving and **tools + camping gear** protected against Seattle's damp per the storage strategy below.

## Objectives (priority order)
1. **Store outdoor implements** — shovels, rakes, hoses, mower, wheelbarrow. Primary driver. Zero storage-condition concern.
2. **Store indoor tools** — hand + power tools. *Needs protection* (see Storage Strategy).
3. **Store camping gear** — tents, bags, cookware, pads. *Mostly fine; two items need indoor storage.*

## Storage Strategy — the humidity question (decided 2026-08-17)
**Key finding:** In Capitol Hill's marine climate, **humidity/condensation is the enemy, not temperature.** An uninsulated shed condenses dew on cold metal nearly every morning for ~8 months/year. That daily damp cycle — not the ambient humidity number — is what rusts tools and mildews gear. It is **not bad enough to require a conditioned outbuilding**, but bad enough that "just add shelves" leaves categories 2 and 3 exposed.

By category:
- **Outdoor implements (obj 1):** No concern. Open shelving / wall hooks. A shed is exactly for this.
- **Indoor tools (obj 2):** Bare steel surface-rusts within a season; power-tool electronics and **Li-ion batteries** degrade in cold + damp.
  - Store in **sealed rolling chest / gasketed totes** with desiccant.
  - **Keep Li-ion batteries indoors** — only bring tools out to the shed.
  - Light oil / VCI (vapor-corrosion-inhibitor) paper on bare metal.
  - *Optional worry-free upgrade:* one insulated/gasketed cabinet with a **Golden Rod dehumidifier rod** (~$40, few watts) inside the shed.
- **Camping gear (obj 3):** Mostly fine in **sealed bins** (bins also solve the rodent problem). Two exceptions **stored indoors**: **down** (sleeping bags/jackets — mildew + loft loss) and **leather/canvas/organic** items.

**Design lever that matters most:** ventilation (soffit/ridge vents so it breathes) + a **raised, sealed floor over a ground vapor barrier**. That reduces condensation far more than conditioning the structure. Spec this, not HVAC.
