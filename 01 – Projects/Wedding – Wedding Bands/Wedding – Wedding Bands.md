# Wedding – Wedding Bands

## Definition of Done
Both wedding bands purchased, sized, and in hand. Needed for Seattle civil ceremony (targeting late Jul / early Aug).

## Jonathan's Band
- **Style:** Chonky yellow gold "dad ring" — classic dome or flat/squared-off profile, no diamonds or ornamentation
- **Width options:**
  - 6mm → ring size 8.75
  - 7mm → ring size TBD (split the difference, try on in person)

#### Other Retailers Checked
- **Frank Darling**: "Dad Band" maxes at 5.5mm ($1,160) — too narrow
- **Just Mens Rings**: 6mm domed $1,300, 8mm $3,435 — overpriced
- **WeddingBands.com**: 7mm $1,422, 8mm $1,632 — decent but pricier than Blue Nile
- **Costco**: Limited to thin bands, no chunky options
- **Etsy/Amazon**: $600-900 range possible but verify solid 14K (not plated/filled)

### Recommendation
**Buy at Blue Nile Portland showroom.** Best price by a meaningful margin. If Blue Nile can't do quarter sizing (need 8.75 for 6mm), Brilliant Earth becomes the fallback despite the premium. A Portland trip lets you hit both showrooms back-to-back.

### Tax Savings — Portland vs Seattle
- WA sales tax on jewelry: ~10.25% (Capitol Hill rate)
- OR sales tax: 0%
- On a $1,000 band = ~$100 saved; on a $1,300 band = ~$133 saved
