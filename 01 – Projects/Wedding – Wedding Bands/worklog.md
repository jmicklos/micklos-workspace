## 2026-06-06 / 2026-06-09 — Pricing research & retailer comparison

### Situation
Jonathan wanted to understand pricing for plain men's 14K yellow gold bands in 6mm, 7mm, and 8mm widths. Wanted to know the best deal, whether Portland (no sales tax) made sense, and specifically whether Blue Nile's Portland showroom was an option.

### Approach & Rationale
Researched pricing across 8+ retailers: Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Frank Darling, Just Mens Rings, WeddingBands.com, Costco, Etsy, Amazon. Blue Nile emerged as best value by a meaningful margin ($981 for 6mm classic dome vs $1,090+ elsewhere). Portland trip makes sense — both Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth have showrooms there, and OR's 0% sales tax saves $100-133 vs Seattle's ~10.25%.

Brilliant Earth was added as a comparison on Jonathan's request. It's 10-20% more expensive but confirmed quarter sizing. This matters because Jonathan's 6mm size is 8.75 (a quarter size).
